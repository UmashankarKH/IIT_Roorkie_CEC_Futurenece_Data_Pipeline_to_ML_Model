## Numeric and date ranges

Important ranges found:

- Orders: quantity `-3` to `5`; discount `0` to `250`; order amount `-1593` to `9576`; delivery time `8` to `1440` minutes.
- Orders: `order_ts` ranges from `2026-09-01 06:30:15` to `2027-01-01 09:00:00`.
- Payments: amount paid `-1593` to `9576`; payment timestamp reaches `2027-01-01 09:01:46`.
- Customers: pincode ranges from `0` to `560102`; signup dates reach `2027-01-05`.
- Products: MRP ranges from `-203` to `883`; cost price ranges from `6.79` to `631.79`.
- Stores: capacity ranges from `0` to `600` orders/day.

Distinct categorical values include invalid or inconsistent values such as `Pending??`, `Cashh`, `cod`, and `UPI ` in orders/payments. Valid business values also include `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`, `RETURNED`, `UPI`, `Card`, `Cash on Delivery`, and `Wallet`. Full distinct-value output is in `profile_summary.csv`.

## Referential-integrity and business-rule results

- Orders reference missing customers, products, and stores; these are critical consistency failures.
- Payments reference eight unknown orders.
- Twelve orders have invalid or duplicate order keys; payments, customers, and products also contain duplicate primary keys.
- Delivered orders are missing delivery times, and some delivery times are 480, 900, or 1,440 minutes.
- Quantities include non-positive values, discounts exceed 100%, and several `order_amount` values do not match the required formula.
- Some order and payment modes are not in the allowed list.
- Fourteen SUCCESS payments do not equal their corresponding order amount.
- Customer emails, phones, and pincodes contain format failures.
- Product categories are missing, one MRP is non-positive, and five products have `cost_price > mrp`.
- Future-dated order and signup records were found.
- One store has no zone, which prevents reliable zone reporting.
- The increment file has 10 existing IDs, all changing from `DELIVERED` to `RETURNED`; these must be merged rather than appended as duplicate facts.

The full row-level register contains 197 entries as of the profiling run date. Increment-file overlap entries are marked `Warning` because they are expected corrections, not bad data; the ELT pipeline must upsert them.

## Questions for the client

1. Should negative quantity or an `order_amount` mismatch be treated as a rejected data-entry error, or can either represent a return/adjustment?
2. When an order changes from DELIVERED to RETURNED, should September revenue be restated historically, or preserved as originally reported with a separate returns view?
3. What retention period and role-based access policy should apply to raw customer PII, quarantine files, and masked reporting tables?
